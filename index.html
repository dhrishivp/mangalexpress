Benvenuti

A proper neighbourhood Italian.

White tablecloths, a red wall, black-and-white film stars looking on, and a kitchen that cooks the Italian classics properly. Rucola has been a fixture of Little Chalfont village for years — somewhere for a quick lunch, a family Sunday or a long dinner with a good bottle of red.

Alongside the menu there’s a daily specials board, with fresh fish a regular feature, and a dessert trolley that makes choosing difficult.

  • Outdoor seating on the terrace in warmer months
  • Families welcome — high chairs available
  • Gluten-free options — please ask your server
  • Pizza to take away — phone ahead to order
